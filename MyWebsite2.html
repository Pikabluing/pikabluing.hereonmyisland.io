This is a dedicated page to all things Barbie: Island Princess. It is the 11th Barbie movie, released to DVD on September 18th, 2007. Barbie stars as Ro, who grew up on a tropical island with only animals, before a prince finds her and introduces her to his kingdom.

Song of the Month

At the Ball

Context

Singers: Tallulah, Sagi, Ro, Tika, Azul

Location: The Castle

Ro's friends are trying to find the best dress for her to wear to impress Prince Antonio at the ball.

Tallulah and Sagi are in conflict about if Ro should wear something that blends into royal society, or if she should stick to her island roots.

In the end, Ro encourages them to compromise and make a dress that blends both aspects together.
